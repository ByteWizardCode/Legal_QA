Legal Question Answering (Legal QA) aims to make complex legal knowledge accessible to diverse users, yet legal data poses significant challenges due to its length, domain-specific terminology, and the presence of multiple, potentially conflicting expert interpretations. We utilize a large-scale dataset from the Vidhikarya platform, comprising real-world legal queries with multiple advocate responses. To address the absence of reliable ground truth, we employ a multi-stage pipeline combining NLI-based contradiction detection, pairwise semantic comparison, and heuristic scoring across informativeness, actionability, and readability, yielding a consistent, high-quality dataset for downstream tasks. Building on this curated data, we propose a unified framework integrating Hierarchical Knowledge Graphs with Reinforcement Learning to enable structured, adaptive, and interpretable legal reasoning. Experiments across Employment and Labour, Criminal, and Family law domains show that our Hierarchical KG+Pastcases+RL framework consistently outperforms all baselines, achieving the highest BERTScore and RougeL across all three scoring dimensions.
